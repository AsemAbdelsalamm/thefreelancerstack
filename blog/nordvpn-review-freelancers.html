NordVPN review for freelancers

Published May 3, 2026 · About 7 minutes

NordVPN is the default name clients recognize when you mention VPNs, which matters when you write guides for non-technical readers. Recognition is not purity, but it reduces friction when you recommend a baseline habit.

Performance is generally solid for consumer use. Like every VPN, results swing by server, time of day, and your ISP mood. Test locally instead of trusting a stranger's speedtest screenshot from 2019.

Features freelancers actually use

Multi-device coverage, reliable killswitch behavior, and specialty servers for specific needs. Do not buy extras you will never toggle. Fancy maps do not pay invoices.

Trust model basics

VPN shifts trust to the provider. Read policy updates when ownership changes. Pair NordVPN with password hygiene and disk encryption when laptops leave the house.

Split tunneling caution

Split tunneling can be handy for local printers while tunneling browser traffic. It can also leak if misconfigured. If you do not understand the setting, leave defaults until you do.

Document what you enable for client environments too. Repeatable setups reduce support calls.

Closing take: NordVPN as a travel habit, not a tattoo

NordVPN is useful when unfamiliar networks are a normal Tuesday and you want consumer-grade simplicity across devices. Read policy updates when ownership shifts, and pair the app with disk encryption and MFA so marketing never oversells what encryption actually fixes.

What I would test on my worst network

I would run uploads, reconnect after sleep, and confirm killswitch behavior on my actual laptop stack. Then open our NordVPN page and Nord vs ExpressVPN before you preach a brand to clients.
