Design tools that respect non-designers

Published May 2, 2026 · About 5 minutes

Most of us are not designers, but clients still judge the visuals. The right tools give structure and files clients can open without drama.

Template-first does not mean generic

Set a basic brand kit once, then reuse it. Consistent layouts make you look organized even on rushed weeks.

Know when to graduate to Figma

When you iterate UI with a developer or share editable components, browser-native design tools beat static slides. Earlier in the funnel, speed beats pixel-perfect precision.

Export checklists save rework

Confirm dimensions for each channel before you animate or illustrate deeply. A five-minute validation beats rebuilding assets because LinkedIn cropped your caption card oddly.
