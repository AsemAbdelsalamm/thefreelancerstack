Trello

Last reviewed: May 2026

★★★★★

Trello is easy to explain to clients. It works best when you want simple boards and low overhead.

Pros

  • Instant understanding for collaborators new to PM software.
  • Power-ups extend calendars, automation, and integrations selectively.
  • Mobile apps feel natural for quick status checks.

Cons

  • Native reporting is lighter than ClickUp-style suites.
  • Card sprawl happens without weekly grooming hygiene.
  • Some power-ups carry extra subscription costs.

Who it is not right for

If you rarely need what Trello is built for, or you are still proving demand before you pay for depth, lighter tools or trials may keep cash and attention freer while you learn your rhythm.

Recommendation

If Trello lines up with the Pros you care about and the Cons sound manageable from how you actually work, it is worth a focused trial on a live client-shaped project rather than a toy sandbox.
