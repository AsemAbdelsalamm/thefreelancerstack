Notion

★★★★★

Notion is great when each client process is different. It can also become a mess if you overbuild.

Pros

  • Linked databases connect tasks, meeting notes, and deliverables.
  • Templates from the community jump-start repeatable engagements.
  • Generous free tier for solo experimentation.

Cons

  • Easy to overbuild, prune ruthlessly each quarter.
  • Offline access historically weaker than native note apps.
  • Very large workspaces need naming discipline to stay searchable.
