ClickUp

★★★★★

ClickUp can run your whole operation, but only if you spend time setting it up.

Pros

  • Multiple views, list, board, calendar, Gantt, without exporting elsewhere.
  • Automation trims recurring PM chores.
  • Growing integrations ecosystem.

Cons

  • Busy interface until you configure defaults aggressively.
  • Setup tax can frustrate freelancers chasing immediate simplicity.
  • Notifications require tuning to avoid noise.
