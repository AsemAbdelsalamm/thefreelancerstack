FREELANCE PLATFORMS

PeoplePerHour

Last reviewed: May 2026

★★★★☆

Smaller marketplace energy with hourlies and fixed offers. Can feel calmer than megasites if your buyers match the regional skew and categories stay stocked.

Pros

  • Hourlies mirror productized thinking without forcing you into one gig layout.
  • Less firehose than the biggest global boards in some disciplines.
  • Reasonable when your timezone and language align with active buyers there.

Cons

  • Volume is thinner; you may still need Upwork-class backup for dry months.
  • Category fit varies; check live demand before you rebuild a portfolio only here.
  • Support and dispute expectations differ; read policy before large deposits move.

Who it is not right for

If you need the widest possible client pool tomorrow, prioritize Upwork or Fiverr first, then treat PeoplePerHour as a side channel that might pleasantly overperform.

Recommendation

Worth listing hourlies that mirror work you already sell elsewhere, then watch conversion for thirty to sixty days before you judge the ceiling. Do not bet the rent on a single small pond.
