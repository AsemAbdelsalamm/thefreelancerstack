FREELANCE PLATFORMS

Fiverr

Last reviewed: May 2026

★★★★☆

Strong when you sell a repeatable slice of work with a clear scope boundary. Weaker when every buyer wants a bespoke maze and compares you only on price.

Pros

  • Packaged gigs train clients to expect a defined output, which cuts negotiation drag.
  • Discovery favors specialists who name problems in plain language buyers actually search.
  • Good place to validate a small offer before you rebuild your whole website around it.

Cons

  • Fee pressure and star visibility can push you toward overdelivery on micro margins.
  • Scope creep still arrives; you need written add-ons, not vibes.
  • Brand perception varies by category; some buyers treat the marketplace like a vending machine.

Who it is not right for

If you only sell long retainers with lots of discovery, or you refuse to productize anything, you will fight the format. Compare Upwork and other platforms before you optimize one profile.

Recommendation

Worth a serious profile if you can list three gigs that ship in predictable time boxes and still feel profitable after fees. Skip it if you need enterprise procurement drama; that rarely maps to gig economics.
