INVOICING & FINANCE

FreshBooks

Last reviewed: May 2026

★★★★★

FreshBooks keeps invoicing and expenses in one place. It is practical and stays out of your way.

Pros

  • Invoices look professional with minimal setup.
  • Time tracking ties billable work directly to line items.
  • Clients get a tidy portal for approvals and payments.

Cons

  • Pricing scales with active clients on many plans, forecast yearly costs.
  • Inventory-heavy businesses may outgrow the sweet spot.
  • Automation depth trails dedicated enterprise stacks.

Who it is not right for

If you rarely need what FreshBooks is built for, or you are still proving demand before you pay for depth, lighter tools or trials may keep cash and attention freer while you learn your rhythm.

Recommendation

If FreshBooks lines up with the Pros you care about and the Cons sound manageable from how you actually work, it is worth a focused trial on a live client-shaped project rather than a toy sandbox.
