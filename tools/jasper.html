Jasper

★★★★☆

Jasper can speed up rough drafts. You still need to edit hard or it sounds generic.

Pros

  • Breaks blank-page paralysis with usable starter paragraphs.
  • Templates map to ads, blogs, and nurture sequences freelancers reuse.
  • Helpful when experimenting with positioning across industries quickly.

Cons

  • Ongoing subscription cost, model against billable hours saved.
  • Always verify facts; hallucinations are your liability with clients.
  • Contracts may require disclosure when AI assists deliverables.
