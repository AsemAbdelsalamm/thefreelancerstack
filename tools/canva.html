Canva

★★★★★

Canva is the fastest way I know to ship usable client graphics. I use it when speed matters more than perfect custom work.

Pros

  • Massive library of layouts sized for real publishing destinations.
  • Brand kits keep palettes and fonts disciplined across client work.
  • Comments and sharing simplify async feedback loops.

Cons

  • Highly recognizable templates if you skip customization.
  • Advanced print workflows may still need specialized tools.
  • Team billing tiers add up as collaborators multiply.
