Hostinger

★★★★★

Hostinger is cheap and usually good enough for freelancer sites. Just watch renewal pricing.

Pros

  • Promotional pricing keeps experiments inexpensive.
  • WordPress tooling and migrations simplify bootstrapping sites.
  • Global data center choices help latency-sensitive readers.

Cons

  • Renewal rates jump, calendar reminders before auto-billing.
  • Upsell flows busy compared to minimalist hosts.
  • Heaviest traffic sites graduate to dedicated infrastructure sooner.
